# Wi‑Fi Troubleshooting Guide - Main Version

## Current Revision

Document Owner: Help Desk Team
Last Updated: September 2026
Priority: Standard

## Overview

This guide provides common troubleshooting steps for wireless connectivity issues.

## Unable to Connect to Wi‑Fi

1. Verify that Wi‑Fi is enabled on the device.
2. Confirm that Airplane Mode is disabled.
3. Ensure the correct wireless network is selected.
4. Restart the device.
5. Attempt to reconnect.

## Known Issues

No issues currently documented.

## Escalation

If the issue persists after all troubleshooting steps have been completed, contact the Service Desk.
## Slow Wireless Connections

If your Wi-Fi connection is slow, try restarting your router. Move closer to the router to improve the signal. Check if too many devices are connected to the network. You can also restart your computer and test your internet connection again.
